Voyagee Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Overview

Voyagee ("the App") is a visual travel assistant that helps travelers understand unfamiliar text, signs, and objects anywhere in the world. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Camera Images: When you take a photo or select an image, it is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini API) for analysis. Images are processed in real-time and are not stored on our servers.

Scan History: Your scan results are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

Ask the Chef Data: Allergen confirmations, spice preferences, and dietary selections are stored temporarily in memory during your session. They are not transmitted or stored permanently.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or location. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the Google Gemini API to analyze images. Images sent for analysis are subject to Google's API Terms of Service. Google does not use paid API inputs to train their models.

Data Storage

All scan history is stored locally on your device. You can delete individual scan records by swiping left on any item in the History tab. You can also delete all data by uninstalling the App. No data is stored on external servers.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository.
