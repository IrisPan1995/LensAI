01
Home

Calm landing. Warm greeting, single CTA, recent scans.

  • PatternGreeting → CTA → History
  • ToneWarm, confident
  • DataSwiftData local
02
Scan

One unified feed — food photos and Chinese text items together. Tap a dish photo for instant AI analysis, or tap a text scenario to enter the live viewfinder with line-by-line detection.

  • FeedPhoto dishes + text scenarios
  • PhotoTap → AI scan animation → Result
  • TextTap → viewfinder → detect → tap word
  • SmartFood → Ask the Chef · Sign → no chef
03
AI Scan

Photo + progress bar + status messages. ~2s simulated analysis.

  • StepsDetect → Identify → Analyze → Context
  • EngineClaude API · base64
04
Result

Cultural explanation — not translation. What, Context, Tips, Allergens. Ask the Chef for real-world confirmation.

  • SectionsWhat · Context · Tips · Alert
  • Honesty"General info — every shop differs"
  • BridgeAsk the Chef
05
Ask the Chef

Hand phone to staff. Bilingual checklist: 12 allergens (3-state), 5 spice levels, 8 dietary items, custom Q&A. Close button to exit anytime.

  • Allergens12 items · yes/no/skip
  • Spice5 levels
  • Dietary8 items · pork, MSG, etc
  • FlowStaff → Thank you → User result
06
History

Personal phrasebook. Search, filter, revisit.

  • StorageSwiftData
  • FilterCategory pills
